## About Faebles
This project is a digital collection of fairy tales created in Fall 2026 as the culminating final project for the ILS-562 course, Digital Libraries, at Indiana University Bloomington (IUB). Many of the digital objects – PDF scans and downloads of fairy tales – are derived from books held by IUB’s vast library resources, notably Wells Library, IU's main library, and the Lilly Library, IU's rare books and manuscripts library. This digital library is intended to work in tandem with existing web collections of fairy tales (i.e.,  [https://fairytalez.com/](https://fairytalez.com/)), which are locatable through a Google search, but whose dynamic website format is unsuited for long term preservation; these are often also purely text-based reproductions, lacking the rich metadata accompanying the PDF as a digital object. This project is also intended to expand and partner with existing scholarly and existing sites (i.e., [https://ufdc.ufl.edu/collections/GRIMM](https://ufdc.ufl.edu/collections/GRIMM)) in a showcase of IUB fairy tale resources that are inclusive of but, importantly, expand beyond a Western cultural context and the IUB libraries – I include the work of the lesser known (but no less important) names such as Frances Jenkins Olcott as well as tales from Asia, the Middle East, and Northern and Eastern Europe. This scope is derived from books, collections, and sources published before 1930 so that they are freely accessible, reproducible, and usable from the public domain. 

While this project is conceived as part of a project for a university graduate course and incorporates what may seem to be high-level concepts and programs such as metadata, coding, GitHub, and CollectionBuilder, the intended users should include members of the general public as would be found in a public library. My intention is to one day work in a public library, and this project would be a useful curated place to point to for patrons interested in reading more fairy tales, for parents looking for stories to tell their children, or for those who want a starting point for researching cultural narratives.
